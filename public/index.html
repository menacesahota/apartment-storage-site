How it works

From drop-off to secure storage, in four simple steps.

Step 1

We drop off boxes

Sturdy plastic storage boxes delivered straight to your building — no need for residents to source their own.

Step 2

Residents pack up

No rush — fill boxes at your own pace with seasonal items, documents, or anything that doesn't fit in a flat.

Step 3

Book a collection

Pick a date on our live calendar. We show real availability, so you always know when we're coming.

Step 4

Secure storage

Boxes are collected and stored safely off-site for as long as you need — 1 month minimum, cancel anytime after.
